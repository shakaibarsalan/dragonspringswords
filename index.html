Our story

Fewer blades, forged properly

Dragon Spring began at a single forge with three smiths and one rule: nothing leaves the workshop unless we would hang it on our own wall. We buy steel by the bar, not the container, and forge in small runs.

It makes us slower and it makes us smaller, and it is the only way we know to make a blade that outlives the person who bought it.
